<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Friedrich Nietzsche was drawing a sharp distinction between actual elevation of spirit and the limited perception of the observer. <p class="wp-block-paragraph">His philosophical contribution centered on questioning conventional morality, individual self-overcoming, and the courage needed to think independently.
